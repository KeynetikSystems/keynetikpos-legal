Privacy Policy

KeynetikPOS  •  Version 1.0

1. What This Covers

This Privacy Policy explains what data the KeynetikPOS App collects, why, and where it goes. It's referenced by, and part of, our Terms of Service.

2. Data That Stays on Your Device

Your product catalogue, prices, stock levels, sales history, staff list, and customer records are stored in a local database on your own phone. We do not collect, see, or have access to this data. If your customers' or staff's personal details (names, phone numbers) end up in this database because you entered them, you are the one responsible for that data under applicable data protection law — not us. If you use "Companion" mode, scans and catalogue changes go to a till you already operate, not to us.

3. Data We Collect — Account and Payments

These only apply if you choose to connect a payment provider for Mobile Money payments — this is optional, and if you never use that feature, none of this section applies to you.

  • Account: the email address and password you use to create an account with us. Your password is hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store it in readable form.
  • Payment provider keys: your Paystack public key (not sensitive) and secret key. The secret key is sent to our server once, stored, and is never sent back to any device or visible to us in the course of normal operation.
  • Payment records: for each Mobile Money charge you initiate, we store the amount, your customer's phone number, a reference number, and the payment status (pending, success, or failed) — enough to complete and confirm the transaction, nothing more.

4. Data We Collect — Advertising

If you're on the free tier, the App shows ads via Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, general location, and other data to select and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy — this data collection is Google's, governed by their terms, not ours. Upgrading to the paid tier turns ads (and this data collection) off entirely.

5. Camera

The App uses your camera to scan barcodes and QR codes. This happens entirely on your device in real time — no photo, video, or image is ever saved or sent anywhere. We only ever receive the decoded barcode text.

6. Why We Use This Data

Account and payment-key data: to let you connect and use your own payment provider account through the App. Payment records: to complete a specific transaction you initiated and let you reconcile it later. We do not use any of this to build an advertising profile of you, and we do not sell personal data to anyone.

7. Where Data Is Stored

Data described in Section 2 never leaves your device unless you explicitly sync it with your own till. Data described in Section 3 is stored on servers operated by our backend infrastructure provider, located outside Kenya — specifically, in the United Kingdom. Your data is protected there by contractual and technical safeguards consistent with Kenya's Data Protection Act's cross-border transfer requirements.

8. Who We Share Data With

  • Paystack: when you initiate a Mobile Money charge, we send them the amount, your customer's phone number, and your account email, using your own Paystack account credentials, so they can process the payment. Paystack's own privacy policy governs what they do with it from there.
  • Our infrastructure provider: hosts the account and payment data described in Section 3, acting on our instructions, not on their own.
  • Google: Play Billing for paid-tier purchases, and AdMob for ads on the free tier (see Section 4).

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

9. How Long We Keep Data

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the App — that's entirely in your control. Account and payment records we hold are kept for as long as your account is active, plus a reasonable period afterward for accounting, dispute, and legal obligations, after which they are deleted.

10. Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, you can ask us to: confirm what data we hold about you; correct it if it's wrong; delete it, subject to any legal obligation we have to keep it; object to how we're using it; or receive a copy in a portable format. Contact us using the details in Section 13. You can also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe we've mishandled your data.

11. Security

Staff PINs are hashed, not stored as plain text. Access to your Paystack secret key is restricted so that only the specific server-side functions that need it to process a charge can reach it. No security measure is perfect, and we can't guarantee data will never be accessed without authorisation — see the Disclaimer of Warranties in our Terms of Service.

12. Children

The App is not directed at, and we do not knowingly collect data from, anyone under 18.

13. Changes and Contact

We may update this Policy from time to time; material changes will ask you to review and accept them again, the same way as our Terms of Service. Questions or requests about your data: keynetiqsystems@gmail.com.
