Delete Your Account

KeynetikPOS

If you connected a KeynetikPOS account for Mobile Money payments, you can request permanent deletion here — no need to have the app installed.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address
  • Any connected payment provider details (Paystack, Direct M-Pesa, or Stripe)
  • Payment records associated with your account

This is permanent and cannot be undone. It does not affect the product catalogue or sales history stored locally on your own device — that data was never sent to us and stays entirely under your control.

Prefer to do it in the app?

Open KeynetikPOS → Settings → Account → Delete My Account. It does exactly the same thing as the request below, immediately.

Request deletion

Email us from the address associated with your account (or tell us which email that is), and we'll delete your account and its data.
